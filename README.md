# 🛡️ Care Transition Efficiency & Placement Outcome Analytics

An end-to-end data analytics and stream-processing web dashboard evaluating the operational velocity, transition bottlenecks, and placement outcomes within the Unaccompanied Alien Children (UAC) care pipeline (CBP $\to$ HHS $\to$ Sponsor Discharge).

---

## 📌 Project Overview
- **Pipeline Scope:** Apprehension & CBP Custody $\to$ HHS Shelter & Case Management $\to$ Sponsor Reunification.
- **Data Coverage:** 720 daily records across 2023–2025.
- **Primary Goal:** Reframe custody volume metrics into actionable process efficiency and throughput indicators.

---

## 🔑 Key Metrics & Formulas
- **Transfer Efficiency Ratio:** $\frac{\text{Transfers out of CBP}}{\text{CBP Custody}} \times 100$
- **Discharge Effectiveness Index:** $\frac{\text{Discharges from HHS}}{\text{HHS Active Care}} \times 100$
- **Pipeline Throughput:** $\frac{\text{Total Discharges}}{\text{Total Apprehensions}} \times 100$
- **Daily Net Backlog:** $\text{Daily Apprehensions} - \text{Daily Discharges}$
